## Content ID

The shelf is not guaranteed clean. On 2026-11-12 the Pixabay catalogue turned out to be almost
entirely "Content ID Registered": of about 90 matching candidates, only four unregistered tracks
were found, and those four are the ones kept here. A registered track may still trigger a claim on
YouTube or Instagram despite the licence; the claim can be disputed with the Pixabay licence page.

Mixkit does not publish a per-track Content ID flag. Its license page offers help with claims
instead, so treat any of the Mixkit tracks as possibly claimable on social platforms.

## Why these tracks

The old shelf was quiet acoustic and piano: warm, but every template sounded the same.
The twenty-four new tracks add the registers the templates actually need — trap and hip-hop
for Reels, funk and disco for friends, drums and tension for the teaser, playful comedy beds
for bloopers, bright short beds for the fifteen-second cut, and strings and piano for the film.
Mood and instrumentation were judged from the catalogue page tags and description, not by
listening.

## Verification

- Full MP3 files, not previews or streams: 44.1 kHz stereo MP3 at 256–320 kbps.
- Durations in the manifest come from ffprobe, not from the page.
- Source pages were checked to resolve and to carry the same track id as the downloaded file;
  the ones under `/free-stock-music/discover/` are the canonical addresses for `mixkit.co/...`.
- The MP3s live under `assets/music/`. The manifest and this file live with the seed in
  `scripts/cicd/seeds/`.
